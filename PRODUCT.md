# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User chose React + Vite + TypeScript. Deploy target: Cloudflare Pages free tier (direct upload, no server, no database). Products must work entirely client-side.

## Users

Groups of 2–12 friends at a restaurant, immediately after the bill arrives, most often on phones, often in dim warm light and under mild social pressure. One person at the table creates the split; the others open a shared link. No account, no app, no training.

## Product Purpose

DinnerSplit turns one restaurant check into fair per-person amounts and the fewest sensible reimbursements in under a minute. Success means the creator reaches a shareable result without hesitation, and every person at the table can verify their own number on their own phone.

## Positioning

A one-night-only splitter: the entire bill, the people, and the settlement live inside a single shareable link. No sign-up, no room to create, no server storage — the result opens instantly for everyone at the table and can be edited by anyone who has the link.

## Operating Context

Used one-handed on a phone at a restaurant table. The bill may be entered as a single total or item by item. Tip and tax are added once for the whole table. One person usually paid the check and needs to be reimbursed. The result is often screenshotted or pasted into WhatsApp / iMessage, and may be revisited after the meal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Add, rename, and remove 2–12 people; names stay client-side.
- Simple total mode: subtotal, tip %, tax as % or amount.
- Itemized mode: items with price, assigned to one or more people; tip and tax distributed proportionally.
- Split methods: equal, custom amount, percentage, by item (itemized mode only).
- Settlement: per-person share, optional payer, minimum transfers, visible tip and tax, exact cent accounting.
- Sharing: full state in the URL, QR code, copied link, copied text summary.
- Privacy: no server-side bill storage; state travels in the URL only.
- English UI, USD formatting. No OCR, AI, accounts, payment processing, currency conversion, or history.
- Suggested defaults from the brief: tip is calculated on the subtotal.

## Brand Commitments

Working name: DinnerSplit. Tone: calm, precise, quietly warm, never jokey at someone's expense. User selected a dark, modern, refined visual direction. The result must be screenshot-worthy.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing brand assets, logo, photography, or testimonials. All illustrative names and amounts are synthetic. Do not invent commercial claims, customer counts, or pricing.

## Product Principles

1. The next action is always obvious; no step asks for information the result does not use.
2. Every number is verifiable by hand, and cents never disappear.
3. The share link is the product: it must open without context and read cleanly on a phone.
4. The evening belongs to the table, not the software; the tool recedes after settling.
5. Privacy is stated plainly, not performed as a feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labeled inputs, ≥4.5:1 body contrast, large touch targets, screen-reader-friendly result structure, reduced-motion support.
